FREQUENTLY ASKED

Got questions?

Everything you need to know before starting your build.

How does pricing work?
Every project is quoted individually based on scope, page count, and whether you want the embedded AI chatbot. Each service comes in a Standard and a Pro tier — Pro adds the custom-trained chatbot, a longer guarantee, and a faster revision window. We’ll walk you through pricing on the free 30-minute blueprint call, or straight over WhatsApp. No obligation.
How long does a website build take?
Landing pages ship in 2–3 business days on Standard and 1–2 on Pro. Corporate websites take 4–6 business days. E-commerce stores take 5–9 business days depending on catalog size.
Do you build bilingual English and Arabic websites?
Yes. Every UTM Solutions build can support bilingual English/Arabic layouts with full right-to-left rendering — mirrored layout, Arabic typography, and RTL-aware components. The embedded AI chatbot replies in both languages and can switch mid-conversation.
How is your AI chatbot different from a widget like Intercom or Tidio?
It's trained on your own content, FAQs, offers and tone, and embedded inside your site using your own design system — no third-party branding, no separate subscription platform, no generic scripts. It answers questions and captures leads 24/7, and escalates to WhatsApp when a human is needed.
Do you use WordPress, Wix, or Squarespace?
No. Every site is hand-coded from scratch rather than built on a drag-and-drop builder or page-builder theme. That keeps page weight low, load times fast, and gives full control over performance, accessibility, and RTL behaviour.
What's included in the free blueprint call?
A 30-minute call where we map your site's structure, discuss your AI chatbot needs, and recommend the right plan — no obligation to continue.
Do I need an existing website?
No. We build from scratch, or we can redesign and upgrade what you already have.
Do you support the site after launch?
Yes. Standard plans include a 14-day guarantee with a 72-hour revision window; Pro plans include a 30-day guarantee with a 24-hour revision window. Ongoing care plans are available for continued updates and monitoring.
Can I upgrade from Standard to Pro later?
Yes. You can move from Standard to Pro at any point as your needs grow — including adding the embedded AI chatbot after your site is already live.
Where are you based and who do you work with?
UTM Solutions LLC is registered in Wyoming, United States, and works with clients remotely worldwide — with particular focus on businesses in the US and the Gulf that need bilingual English/Arabic websites.
